Usual flow
Four steps, one go
Select a sentence, click once, and the result lands next to you — without leaving the conversation.
01
Select a passage
Highlight the line you want to use.
02
Click ✦ on the strip
The second cell of the edge strip slides the panel out.
03
Pick an action
Quote it, keep it as a note, or hand it to the model to rewrite, summarise or translate.
04
It lands next to you
Into the composer, the sticky wall, or your snippets — nothing is sent automatically.
The same four steps work for a one-line note: ✎ → note / follow-up / reminder → Enter.
